Beta version · last updated 29 October 2026

Who runs this game

Digby: Dig to the Core is made by Saerens Advertising (Belgium, BE 1019.436.742). Questions: axel@saerensadvertising.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (depth, XP, relics, upgrades, settings) is stored in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device unless you copy a save code yourself. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Gameplay statistics (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager) to see how the beta is played: for example how far players dig, which upgrades they buy and whether they come back. We use this only to improve the game. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not ask for your name or email. If you decline, Google tags only send cookieless, anonymous signals without identifiers (Google Consent Mode).

Your choices

You can change your choice at any time through the cookie settings. You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
