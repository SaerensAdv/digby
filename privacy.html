Beta version · last updated 29 October 2026

Who runs this game

Digby: Dig to the Core is made by Saerens Advertising (Belgium, BE 1019.436.742). Questions: axel@saerensadvertising.com.

Game progress

Your depth, XP, relics, upgrades and settings are stored in this browser's local storage. Digby does not send game progress to our servers. Clearing this site's browser data removes it. If you export and share a save code, you share its contents yourself.

Consent banner

We use ConsentEase to show the cookie banner, apply your consent choices and keep consent records. ConsentEase processes consent choices and related technical information on our behalf. See ConsentEase's privacy policy and data processing agreement. You can change or withdraw your choice through the banner's preferences.

Optional game analytics

Digby is prepared to send gameplay events to Google Analytics 4 (GA4) through Google Tag Manager (GTM), but those event tags are not active until the GTM configuration is imported and published. In this beta, Digby requests the GTM container only after ConsentEase reports that you granted Analytics consent. If you decline, GTM is not loaded. If you later withdraw Analytics consent, Digby stops queuing gameplay events and the GA4 event tag requires analytics consent. A GTM script that was already loaded is not removed from the page. After the GTM configuration is published, events may include the game version, form, level, depth, upgrades and session activity. No save-code contents, name or email are sent.

Your rights

For privacy questions or requests, contact axel@saerensadvertising.com. You can also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
