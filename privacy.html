Who runs this game

Digby: Dig to the Core is made by Saerens Advertising (Belgium, BE 1019.436.742). Questions: axel@saerensadvertising.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (depth, XP, relics, upgrades and settings) is stored in this browser's local storage. It stays on your device; clearing this site's browser data removes it. If you export and share a save code, you share its contents yourself.

Analytics

Analytics are not active in the current beta. The game does not currently load Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager, and Digby does not send your game progress to our servers. A consent banner has not been enabled yet. If analytics are added in a later beta update, we will enable consent controls before loading analytics tags and update this notice with the exact data and choices.

Your rights

For privacy questions, contact axel@saerensadvertising.com. You can also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
